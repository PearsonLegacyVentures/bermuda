What to share about your setting

Bermuda’s property policies and island transport constraints should be considered before offering a confirmed appointment. The most useful information is a named accommodation, exact entrance, permission for a visiting therapist and a suitable private treatment room.

Choosing a treatment

Guests may enquire about Swedish relaxation massage, deep tissue massage or couples massage. A two-person session requires two professionals and suitable space; none is guaranteed by submitting a form.

Before you commit

BERMUDA · PAGET

Before requesting a massage in Paget

Paget is a central Bermuda parish with residential properties and accommodation close to the harbour. A hillside private residence and a waterfront guesthouse may have different entrance and parking requirements.

Property entry and privacy

Guests should clarify the exact guest property, road entrance and whether a private sheltered room can accommodate equipment. Residential hosts and hotel staff may have separate rules for outside visitors.

Choosing the treatment

An individual relaxing massage may suit a longer stay, while couples and group wellness need private space and confirmed local professional capacity. An open lawn or pool area is not automatically appropriate.

Location and travel

Paget's proximity to Hamilton does not mean the same arrival procedure or rate. Identify the property's actual driveway or reception and preferred local appointment time.

Paget guest question

Can a therapist attend a Paget private residence? It can be requested if the host permits the visit and a qualified local professional can be confirmed for that address.
